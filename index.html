How We Extract Purity

Retaining the golden tradition of South Indian cold-pressed goodness.

01

Selection of Seeds

We handpick sun-dried groundnuts, copra coconuts, and clean sesame seeds directly from trusted local farmers.

02

Traditional Wood Press

Crushed slowly in authentic Vaagai tree wood expellers without generating heat, preserving nutrients and aroma.

03

Natural Sun Sedimentation

No chemical bleaching. Oils are settled naturally under sunlight and double filtered through organic cotton cloth.

04

Hygienic Bottling

Packed in sterilized, food-grade bottles ready to elevate your family's cooking and spiritual traditions.
